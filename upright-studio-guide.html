Signing in

Your admin area is at this address. Save it as a bookmark on your phone and laptop.

uprightconceptsmedia.com/admin
  1. Open the address above and enter your email (admin@uprightconceptsmedia.com) and password.
  2. You land on the Dashboard. It shows new enquiries, how many albums and photos are live, and whether packages and testimonials are showing.
  3. Use the menu on the left to move around. On a phone, tap the menu icon at the top left.

When you are done, open the menu at the bottom left (your email) and choose Sign out, especially on a shared computer.

Enquiries

Every message sent through the Book a shoot form on your website lands in Enquiries. New ones show a gold number in the menu.

  1. Tap an enquiry to read it. It is marked Read automatically.
  2. Tap Reply on WhatsApp to open WhatsApp with a greeting already written. Call and Email work the same way.
  3. Set the status to Replied once you have answered, or Archived when it is finished. The tabs at the top filter by status.

Use the search box to find someone by name, phone number or anything they wrote.

Albums and photos

Each shoot or event is an album. Albums appear on the Work page, and the ones you star also appear on the homepage.

Add a new album

  1. Go to Albums and tap New album. Give it a title and pick a category.
  2. On the album screen, tap Add photos, or drag photos from your computer onto the photo area. You can select many at once.
  3. Keep the page open while the photos upload. Each one is saved as soon as it finishes.
  4. Fill in the Details on the right: a short summary, the story, location and date. Tap Save details.
  5. Switch on Published to put the album on the website. New albums stay hidden until you do.

Arrange and tidy

  • Drag photos by the grip in their top-left corner to change the order.
  • Tap the ⋯ button on a photo to Set as cover, add a description and caption, or delete it.
  • Tap the Feature star to show the album on the homepage. Six featured albums look best.
  • On the Albums list, drag whole albums to change their order on the Work page.
  • To add a video, paste a YouTube or Vimeo link under Videos.
Replace the sample photos. The website launched with sample photos from a free photo library so it would look complete. Replace them with your own work album by album, then delete the sample albums you no longer need.

Words on your pages

Page content holds the headlines, text and photos for each page. Use the tabs to pick a page, then edit a section and tap its Save button. Each section saves on its own.

  • Homepage: the big headline and rotating photos, the introduction, the "From shoot to frame" steps, the drone section (paste a YouTube link to show a play button) and the closing section.
  • About: your story, your portrait, key facts and how you work. Please replace the sample facts with real ones.
  • Contact, Work page and Services page: the heading and short text at the top of each.

If you leave a page with unsaved changes, the admin asks before throwing them away.

Services, packages and testimonials

These three work the same way: tap an item to edit it, drag to reorder, use the switch to hide it without deleting, or tap the bin to delete.

  • Services: the nine things you offer, each with a photo and description.
  • Packages: names, prices and what is included. Write prices the way you want them shown, for example "From ₦150,000". Tap Highlight to give one package a gold accent.
  • Testimonials: short quotes from clients. The samples are placeholders, so replace them with real words from real clients.

Your password

  • To change it: open Settings, scroll to Password, enter your current password and the new one twice. Use at least 10 characters with a letter and a number.
  • If you forget it: on the sign-in page tap Forgot password?. A link arrives at admin@uprightconceptsmedia.com. It works once and expires after an hour.

Good habits

  • Changes appear within about a minute. If the website still shows the old version, wait a moment and refresh.
  • Upload your best export. Full-size JPGs straight from your editing app are ideal. Very large files are shrunk automatically, and the website serves the right size to every phone.
  • Describe your photos. A plain description such as "Bride and groom dancing at their Lekki reception" helps Google show your work in image search.
  • Keep albums focused. Fifteen strong photos sell better than eighty similar ones.
  • Check enquiries daily. A quick reply is often what wins the booking.
